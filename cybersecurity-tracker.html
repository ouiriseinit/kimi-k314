Adaptations made: Python/C minimized — CS50 gives you the conceptual foundation in C, then we pivot to JS/Node for all tooling. Your existing WSL/Kali/Docker knowledge accelerates Weeks 3–4. Web security is emphasized because your stack (HTML, CSS, JS, Express) is the battlefield. Complexity Scale mapped: Test Drive = conceptual exposure, Utility = CLI/networking, Component = builds and projects.
